隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

答案之书 Duo（以下称“本应用”）由个人开发者 MIDJUG 制作。本应用不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息。

  • 不需要注册或登录账号。
  • 不联网。答案在你的设备上随机抽取，不会发送到任何服务器。
  • 没有广告，没有统计分析工具，也没有第三方 SDK。
  • 不读取通讯录、照片、位置、麦克风、相机或健康数据。
  • 本应用读取 iPhone Duo 的铰链状态和角度，只用来判断你是打开、合上还是翻页。这些数据只在设备上使用，不会保存或发送。

本应用的源代码公开在 GitHub，任何人都可以查看以上说法。

如果这份政策将来有变化，会更新在本页并修改生效日期。有问题请在 GitHub Issues 留言。

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Answer Book Duo (“the app”) is made by MIDJUG, an independent developer. The app does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No network access. Answers are picked at random on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or health data.
  • On iPhone Duo the app reads the hinge state and angle only to tell whether you opened, closed or flicked the phone. This stays on the device and is never saved or sent.

The source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check these statements.

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated. Questions are welcome in GitHub Issues.
